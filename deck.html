TL;DR

The app makes Ramesh start from nothing. We put his products, his past orders and a clear bill first.

Problem

Once a month Ramesh rebuilds a long list from memory, reads it out on calls, pays a large amount and has no list to check what arrives.

Insight · assumption

He restocks mostly the same items and knows his list. The app should make finding and adding fast, not guess for him.

Solution

A products-first app: search, categories and Order again on home, quantity in place, one clear bill with khata, a checklist on arrival. Kari, a voice assistant, is one call button in the nav.

How we will know

Time from app open to order placed, orders started from Order again or search, missing items reported on arrival.

The ask

Approve a four step validation: shop visits, a voice and photo test, a click-through test with this prototype, then a one district pilot.

UI and UX principles

Eight rules every screen was checked against

1

One primary action

Each screen has a single orange button. Everything else is outlined or a link.

2

Recognition over recall

Real pack photos, last quantity and past orders. He never has to remember.

3

Numbers before words

Total, price and margin lead. Sentences are short and optional.

4

Progressive disclosure

Home is the catalogue. Help stays one tap away, never in front of products.

5

Forgiving by default

Steppers, Empty cart, a 2 minute edit window, and Kari never places the order.

6

Visible system status

Chips say what is applied. Tracking has four plain steps. Every tap answers back.

7

Consistency

Price pill, add button and header match the live app, so nothing must be relearned.

8

Access for everyone

Hindi script and English, explain and listen buttons, voice and camera input, dark text on light.

Language · the user decides the design

For this user, language is the product

Ramesh reads Hindi first and little English. If a word is not understood, the feature behind it does not exist.

Proper Hindi, proper script

Every screen is written in Devanagari and set in Noto Sans Devanagari, with taller line height so conjuncts stay intact.

English, one tap away

Both languages are complete. He picks on the first screen and can switch any time.

Regional languages next

Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil and Punjabi are shown on the first screen as coming soon.

A "?" that explains

Margin, bulk price, khata, wallet and Kari open a short explanation that can be read aloud.

Words he already uses stay as they are: ऑर्डर, बिल, स्कीम, मार्जिन. Numbers stay in Western digits, as on his price tags. The Hindi needs a native writer's review.

Live · 02 Order · Kari

Kari: say the order, or show the parchi

  • One button in the nav, purple with a slow pulse. It never covers prices or products.
  • Speaks his way. "Atta 8 bori kar do aur 2 peti sarson tel" is understood as said.
  • A slip with pictures. Each item has a photo and a stepper to correct it.
  • Asks when unsure. Out of stock? It offers a swap. It also answers rates and order status.
  • Reads his parchi and the delivery bill from a photo, and asks when a line is unclear.
  • Never places the order. The slip goes to his cart; he makes the final tap.

The voice understanding runs in the browser. Photo results are sample data and need a vision model.

Validation plan · cheapest test first

Four steps from assumption to evidence

Week 1

Talk to retailers

8 to 10 shop visits. How the list is made, how often, how they pay.

Learn: is the order really a repeat?

Week 2

Voice and photo test

Retailers say a list to a Kari mock and photograph their real parchi.

Learn: how often an item is wrong.

Week 3

Click-through test

This prototype, both journeys, unaided, on the retailer's own phone.

Learn: can he find and order alone? Which language?

Weeks 4 to 8

One district pilot

Real orders, with Order again and Call Kari behind a flag.

Learn: the baseline for every metric.

Go or no-go after each step.

Risks

What could go wrong, and the fallback

Kari mishears or misreads

Pictures confirm each item. Search and browse always work without Kari.

Order again repeats a mistake

Quantities are editable in place, and nothing is ordered without his tap.

Feed use drops

The feed is one tap away. We measure it and keep what retailers use.

Khata is not approved

The flow still works with UPI and cash.

Breaking distributor trust

Pilot with distributors already on the platform.

Needs a business decision

Lending partner and limits, ETA data from logistics, voice and photo running cost, regional language copy.
